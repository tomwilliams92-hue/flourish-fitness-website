Common questions

When can I start after having my baby?

From 6 weeks after birth, or 8 to 10 weeks after a C-section, once you feel ready to exercise again. Go at whatever pace feels right for you.

Which classes can my baby come to?

MumFit Social (babies up to 3), MumFit Lift and MumFit Lift+. Bring a car seat, pram or blanket for them. Women's HIIT is baby-free, so it's an hour for you.

I'm pregnant. Can I join?

Yes, MumFit Preglates is for every stage of pregnancy. Please don't come if a healthcare professional has told you not to exercise.

I've never done Pilates. Is that OK?

Yes. Thursday Pilates suits complete beginners as well as people who've done it before.

What should I bring?

A water bottle and clothes you can move in. For Pilates, a pillow and blanket for the relaxation if you'd like. Let Amy know about any injuries before your first class.

Can I get a refund or swap my place?

Bookings can't be refunded or transferred to someone else.
